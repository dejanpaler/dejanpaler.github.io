Dejan Paler — Java Developer and Architect

Java developer and architect since 2005. I design and build Java backend services and their frontends, and modernize systems already in production. I work as a freelancer, remotely from Slovenia.

Most of my work has been in the financial services domain: leasing, lending and factoring; payments and merchant onboarding; prepaid cards and money transfer. I have also worked in regulated gaming, search engine optimization, and backup and recovery.

Consulting and development

On a new system I start with the architecture: design documents, decision records and proofs of concept that the client's team uses to choose its technologies. Security is part of the design from the start. Then I build the backend services and the frontends (in plain JavaScript, React, Angular or JSF). I integrate the system with the ones around it, such as payment and KYC providers, credit bureaus, e-signature and ERP systems. The systems run on platforms such as OpenShift, Kubernetes or AWS. When a system in production needs it, I migrate its functionality from the old platform to a modern one.

I build observability into the system, so incidents are traced to their root cause sooner and performance problems are easier to find. I fix both. In the client's team I introduce code reviews, automated code analysis, test automation and continuous delivery, and I train and mentor developers. Spec-driven and AI-assisted development are part of how I work every day.

I work on a project basis or on time and materials.
