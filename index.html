Dejan Paler — Java Developer and Architect

Java developer and architect since 2005. I design and build Java backend services and their frontends, and modernize systems already in production. I work as a freelancer, remotely from Slovenia.

Most of my work has been in the financial services domain: leasing, lending and factoring; payments and merchant onboarding; prepaid cards and money transfer. I have also worked in regulated gaming, search engine optimization, and backup and recovery.

Consulting and development

On a new system I start with the architecture: design documents, decision records and proofs of concept that your team uses to choose its technologies. Security is part of the design. Then I build the backend services (in Java SE, Quarkus, Java EE or Spring Boot), the frontends (in plain JavaScript, React, Angular or JSF) and the integrations with external services, such as payment and KYC providers, credit bureaus, e-signature and ERP systems. The system runs on your platform, such as AWS, Kubernetes or OpenShift.

Spec-driven and AI-assisted development are part of how I work every day, and so is automated testing. I diagnose and fix incidents and performance problems in production. To find their cause sooner, I build observability into the system.

When a system in production needs it, I migrate its functionality from the old platform to a modern one. In your team I introduce code reviews, automated code analysis and continuous delivery, and I train and mentor your developers.

I work on a project basis or on time and materials.
